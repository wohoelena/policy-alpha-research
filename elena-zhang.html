Founder Profile

Elena Zhang

Founder & Research Lead · Policy Alpha Research

Elena Zhang leads Policy Alpha Research, an independent platform examining how policy shifts, industrial transformation, and capital rotation affect sectors, company fundamentals, valuation, and market pricing.

Research Perspective

Her work connects macro policy, industrial strategy, sector fundamentals, and market confirmation across themes including AI infrastructure, critical minerals, energy systems, ESG capital access, strategic assets, and thematic equities.

Research is structured around a repeatable process: identify the policy signal, map the transmission path, assess company quality and valuation, and define observable conditions that could confirm or invalidate the thesis.

The objective is not to predict every market move. It is to build evidence-based frameworks that make structural change, valuation risk, and capital-allocation consequences easier to evaluate.

Research Governance

How the Work Is Developed

01Source Review

Public filings, regulator publications, company releases, and cited third-party evidence.

02Transmission Map

Policy direction is mapped through sectors, supply chains, capital expenditure, and company economics.

03Valuation Context

Market expectations are separated from operating evidence, scenario assumptions, and risk.

04Ongoing Review

Material policy, financial, or market changes trigger thesis review and, where needed, correction.
